    Table

      Use mouse or touch to play cards and draw from the pile. Keyboard shortcuts on the game board: left and right arrows move between cards, Enter or Space plays selected card, D draws a card, P reads the top discard, question mark opens keyboard help.

      Mouse, touch, and keyboard are all supported.

      Keyboard Commands

      • Left Arrow: select previous card
      • Right Arrow: select next card
      • Enter or Space: play selected card
      • D: draw a card
      • P: announce top discard card
      • C: announce selected card
      • H: announce your full hand
      • ?: open this help
      • Escape: close this help
