    Table

      Use mouse or touch to play cards and draw from the pile. Keyboard shortcuts on the game board: left and right arrows move between cards, Home and End jump to the first or last card, Enter or Space plays selected card, D draws a card, P reads the top discard, T announces whose turn it is and the discard, C announces your card count, S announces your score, Shift+S announces every score, R Y B G jump to the highest card of that color, Shift with those keys jumps to the lowest, question mark opens keyboard help.

      Mouse, touch, and keyboard are all supported.

      Keyboard Commands

      • Left Arrow: select previous card
      • Right Arrow: select next card
      • Home: select first card
      • End: select last card
      • Enter or Space: play selected card
      • D: draw a card
      • P: announce top discard card
      • T: announce whose turn it is and the top discard
      • C: announce how many cards are in your hand
      • H: announce your full hand
      • S: announce your score
      • Shift+S: announce every player's score, highest to lowest
      • R / Shift+R: select highest / lowest red card
      • Y / Shift+Y: select highest / lowest yellow card
      • B / Shift+B: select highest / lowest blue card
      • G / Shift+G: select highest / lowest green card
      • ?: open this help
      • Escape: close this help
